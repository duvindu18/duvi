VEIL - FACE ANONYMIZATION FOR WINDOWS
=====================================

Please read these notices before installing. By continuing you confirm that
you will use face recognition lawfully.

1. RESPONSIBLE USE
   Face images and face embeddings are biometric personal data. In many
   jurisdictions (for example the EU GDPR, Article 9, and the Illinois BIPA)
   recognising identifiable people requires a legal basis or their explicit
   consent. Veil processes everything locally: no images, videos or face data
   are uploaded anywhere. Only the model and runtime files you select are
   downloaded during setup.
