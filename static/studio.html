The studio

A small studio forbuildings that don'texist yet.

Studio Morfis was founded in 2020. In 6 years we have handled local and international projects, from small private houses to large infrastructure, for architects, engineers, developers and public clients.

We make still images, films and interactive walkthroughs. The tools change; the questions stay the same: where the light comes from, how a material feels, and how a place will be used.

Principles

  1. 01

    Start from the drawings

    Every image is built from the architect's own plans, sections and models. We do not invent what has not been designed.

  2. 02

    Light before detail

    We set the sun, the sky and the hour before placing a single object. Most of an image is decided by its light.

  3. 03

    One image, one idea

    Each view should say one thing about the building. A few precise images serve a project better than many.

  4. 04

    Honest materials

    Concrete, timber and stone keep their real grain, weathering and imperfection, so a design can be judged as it will be built.
