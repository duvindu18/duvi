The studio

A small office forbuildings that taketheir time.

Studio Morfis was founded in 2011 by Astrid Morfis. We are fourteen people working on a deliberately small number of projects at a time.

Our work spans private houses, cultural and sacred buildings, and public housing. The scale changes; the questions stay the same: mass, light, and how a room will feel in forty years.

Principles

  1. 01

    Subtract before adding

    We begin by asking what a building can do without. Most of our best rooms are made by removing.

  2. 02

    Let structure finish

    Where the structure can be the surface, we let it. Fewer layers age more honestly.

  3. 03

    Draw with light

    Every plan is also a drawing of the sun's path. Openings are placed for an hour, not an elevation.

  4. 04

    Build near

    Materials are sourced as close to site as practical: earth from the excavation, stone from the valley.
