*Effective: [DATE]. Developer: [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [CONTACT EMAIL].*

Veil Cam is a camera app that blurs faces on the phone. It is built so that your photos,
videos and the people in them never leave your device.

## What the app processes, and where

- **Camera and microphone.** Used only to show the viewfinder and to record the photos
  and videos you take. Face detection, depth, person outlines and the palm gesture are
  computed on your phone, in real time. No image, video, audio or face data is sent
  anywhere, and the app has no internet permission.
- **Location (optional).** Only if you turn on *Embed GPS*. Your location is then written
  into the metadata of the clips you record, on your phone, and nowhere else. Turning
  the setting off stops it; you can also deny the permission.
- **Your photos and videos** are stored on your phone: in the app's private library or,
  if you choose, in the phone's gallery. You decide whether to share or export them.

## Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play. Veil Cam receives from Google Play only
a confirmation that your account owns the purchase, and keeps that confirmation on your
phone so the app works offline. We do not receive your payment details. Google's
privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What we collect

Nothing. Veil Cam has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no servers. We do
not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

## Children

Veil Cam is not directed at children under 13.
