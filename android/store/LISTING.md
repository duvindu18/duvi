**Short description** (max 80):

**Full description** (max 4000):

Veil Cam is a pro camera that helps keep the people in your shots private. Faces are
found and blurred on the phone, frame by frame, before anything is saved: each frame
waits for its face detection instead of guessing. Nothing is uploaded, and the app has
no internet access. (Like any detector, it can miss very small, distant or heavily
covered faces; check important footage before you share it.)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Face blur, pixelate, black-out or your own mask, on every frame of the recording
• Follows faces at any angle, including tilted and turned heads
• Choose to hide every face, or only the one or two most certain ones
• Private library inside the app; export only what you choose

CINEMATIC VIDEO
• Cinematic depth with real depth estimation: subject sharp, background in lens blur
• Tap a person or object to focus on it; the focus follows them
• Programmable A-B focus pulls with duration and easing curve
• Bokeh shapes: round, six-blade, anamorphic, swirl
• Shutter angle, 24/25/30/48/50/60 fps, HEVC or H.264, bitrate up to Max
• LUTs on the monitor or baked in, film grain, halation and bloom
• Anamorphic de-squeeze monitoring (1.33x, 1.55x, 2.0x)
• Pre-record: keep the 3–10 seconds before you press record
• Reel/clip file names (A001_C001_YYYYMMDD) with lens, ISO, shutter angle, white
  balance and optional GPS in the clip's metadata and a sidecar file

SMART PHOTOS
• Multi-frame merge for clean night shots and HDR, without ghosting
• Scene recognition picks the right merge automatically
• Framing suggestions, grid and level

HANDS-FREE AND GIMBALS
• Show an open palm to start and stop recording, with a countdown
• Gimbal and Bluetooth remote buttons for record, zoom and more; learn any button

PRO MONITORING
• Zebras, focus peaking, false color, histogram, audio meters
• Manual ISO, shutter, white balance, tint and focus; AE/AF lock

TRY IT, THEN BUY ONCE
Download free to try the viewfinder and every setting. Taking photos and recording
unlock with a single one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads.

Requires Android 11 or later and a 64-bit phone. Features such as 4K, 60 fps, HEVC and
manual controls depend on what your phone's camera supports.
