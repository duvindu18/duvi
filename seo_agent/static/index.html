1 · Check keywords with real Fiverr & Upwork data

  1. Load keywords for your niche, or add your own.
  2. For each one, click Fiverr. Note the number of results, and whether the phrase appears in Fiverr's search suggestions when you type its first words.
  3. Click Jobs and Talent on Upwork and note the counts.
  4. Type the numbers in. The list re-ranks itself, and the Built-in engine uses your best-scoring keywords as tags.

The app only opens normal search pages in your browser. It never reads or scrapes Fiverr or Upwork, which their rules forbid. Everything you enter is saved on this computer only.

Score (0-100): demand (in Fiverr's suggestions, open Upwork jobs) against competition (number of gigs or freelancers, on a log scale). It's a rule of thumb for comparing your own keywords, not an official metric.

2 · Track your gig stats

On Fiverr, open your gig's Analytics and copy impressions, clicks and orders for the same period each time (for example, the last 14 days). Note what you changed, then compare the next entry.
