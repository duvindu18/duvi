## Full description (max 4000)
CADView opens AutoCAD drawings and SketchUp models on your phone or tablet, without an internet connection and without an account.

Everything happens on your device. Files are never uploaded, so you can open drawings from clients and site teams without worrying about where they end up.

FILES
• DWG from AutoCAD R12 up to 2018 and later
• DXF (ASCII)
• SketchUp SKP (2013 and later)
• 3D models: GLB/glTF, OBJ, STL, PLY, DAE, 3MF
• Open from email, WhatsApp, Drive or your file manager with Share or Open with

DRAWINGS
• Layers with colours, line types (dashed, centre, hidden) and filled areas
• Hatch patterns, dimensions, leaders and arrows
• Text and multi-line text with the drawing's text styles and wrapping
• Blocks, including nested, rotated and arrayed blocks
• Find text anywhere in the drawing
• Monochrome view for reading busy drawings

MEASURE AND MARK UP
• Distances and areas, snapping to ends, midpoints, corners and centres
• Hold a placed point to move it
• Notes pinned to the drawing
• Units: mm, cm, m, inches and feet, with the drawing's own unit detected

COMPARE REVISIONS
• Put two versions of a DWG or DXF on top of each other and see what was added and removed

3D MODELS
• Orbit, pan and zoom with one and two fingers
• Show and hide tags and components
• Section cuts and standard views
• Large models with many repeated components stay smooth

EXPORT
• PDF on A4 to A1, or a high-resolution PNG
• Whole drawing, the visible area or an area you select
• Colour or monochrome on white, with an approximate scale

BUILT FOR PHONES AND TABLETS
• Pinch, double-tap and two-finger tap zoom
• Portrait and landscape, phones, foldables and tablets
• Light mode and a true-black dark mode
• Handles big files, including drawings with hundreds of thousands of objects

Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
