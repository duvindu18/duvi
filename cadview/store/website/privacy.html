CADView

Privacy policy

Last updated: [[DATE]]

CADView is a viewer for CAD drawings and 3D models, published by [[DEVELOPER NAME]]. This policy covers the CADView app for Android and iOS.

Short version

CADView doesn't collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. It works without an internet connection and has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your files

Drawings and models you open are read and displayed on your device. They are never uploaded. To keep a list of recent files, the app saves its own copy of each opened file in its private storage on your device. Removing a file from the list deletes that copy. Your original file is never changed or deleted.

Notes, measurements, the last view of each file and your settings (theme, units) are stored in the same private app storage. They stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Sharing and exporting

When you export a PDF or image, or share notes or a file, CADView hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice and is covered by the privacy policy of the app you send it to.

Permissions

  • Files: CADView only sees files you pick in the system file picker or send to it with "Open with" or "Share". It does not scan your storage.
  • Vibration (Android): short haptic ticks when snapping to a point.

The app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo library access.

Data collected

None. There is no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app. If the app store you downloaded from (Google Play or the App Store) shows you crash or usage statistics, those are collected by Google or Apple under their own policies and shared with developers only in anonymous, aggregated form, if you have agreed to it in your device settings.

Children

CADView is a general audience app for working with technical drawings. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, if it adds optional ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
