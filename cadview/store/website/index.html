Common questions

Which files can it open?

DWG (AutoCAD R12 to 2018 and later), DXF (ASCII), SketchUp SKP (2013 and later), and 3D models in GLB/glTF, OBJ, STL, PLY, DAE and 3MF.

How do I open a file from email, WhatsApp or Drive?

Tap the attachment, then choose Share or Open with and pick CADView. You can also open files from inside the app with Open file.

Does it upload my drawings?

No. Everything happens on your device and the app doesn't use the internet.

Some text or hatch patterns look different from AutoCAD

CADView draws text with one built-in font, so SHX and TrueType fonts are approximated by width. Very dense hatch patterns are drawn as a light tint so the drawing stays fast. Paper space layouts (sheets) aren't shown yet; the model space drawing is. You can export the whole drawing, the visible area or a selected area to PDF.

My SketchUp model doesn't open, or shows only a preview image

Large SketchUp files may not work on a phone: very big or complex models can run out of memory, and a few SketchUp features aren't supported by the reader yet. In SketchUp, use File → Export → 3D Model and choose .dae or .glb, then open that file.

Removing a file from the list

This deletes only the app's copy. The original file on your device or in the cloud isn't touched.
