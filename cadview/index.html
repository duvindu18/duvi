    Welcome to CADView

    A few things worth knowing before you start.

    Gestures

    Gestures

    • DragMove around
    • PinchZoom in and out
    • TwistRotate the drawing
    • Double-tapZoom in
    • Two-finger tapZoom out
    • HoldOptions. While measuring: magnifier. On a placed point: move it.

    Good to know

    • Offline and private. Files are opened on this device and never uploaded.
    • Opening files. Use Open a file, or in another app tap Share or Open with and choose CADView.
    • DWG files are converted on the device. A large one can take several seconds the first time; after that it opens straight away.
    • Large SketchUp files may not open. Very big or complex .skp models can run out of memory on a phone, and some SketchUp features aren't supported yet. If a model doesn't open, or shows only its preview, export it from SketchUp as .glb or .dae (File → Export → 3D Model) and open that.
    • Not shown yet: paper-space layouts (sheets) and line weights. The model space drawing is shown.
    • Removing a file from the list deletes only the app's copy, never your original.

    Gestures are always available under More → Gestures.
